Privacy policy

Your information stays on your device.

One-Fill is a local-first autofill extension. There is no account, no server and no analytics. This page explains exactly what One-Fill stores, what it can access, and what it never does.

What One-Fill stores

  • The profile details you enter (name, contact, address, education, professional details, IDs, emergency contacts).
  • Resume files you upload, stored as files in the extension’s storage.
  • Your settings, and mapping corrections you confirm per website (a field fingerprint → which profile field it is — never a value).
  • Optional form history: website domain, page title, date and number of fields filled. Never the values you filled or full page URLs.

Everything is kept in chrome.storage.local on this device. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

What One-Fill never does

  • Send your data to any server — One-Fill makes no network requests at all.
  • Use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • Share, sell or upload your profile.
  • Silently fill sensitive fields such as PAN, Aadhaar, passport, driving licence or voter ID.
  • Fill passwords, one-time codes, captchas, payment card or bank details, or tick consent boxes.
  • Log your profile values to the browser console.

How pages are accessed

By default One-Fill can only read a page after you click its icon or press its shortcut (Chrome’s activeTab permission). It then looks at the form fields on that page — labels, names and field types — to decide what each field asks for. Values already typed into the page are not read, only whether a field is empty.

If you turn on Detect forms as pages load or Auto-fill safe fields, Chrome asks you to grant access to the sites you visit. You can revoke it at any time from Settings or chrome://extensions.

How your profile is protected

  • Storage is restricted to One-Fill’s own pages. Scripts running inside websites cannot read your profile.
  • A website only receives the specific values for the fields you choose to fill.
  • Sensitive values are masked in the popup and in the editor until you reveal them.
  • In-page messages are rendered in a closed shadow root and never display your data.
  • Exports exclude ID numbers unless you opt in, and can be encrypted with a password (AES-256-GCM).

Permissions

PermissionWhy
storageKeep your profiles, settings and history on this device.
activeTabAccess the current tab only when you invoke One-Fill.
scriptingRun the form detector and filler in that tab.
Site access (optional)Only if you enable page-load detection or safe auto-fill.

One-Fill does not request tabs, history, bookmarks, cookies or downloads.

Your controls

You can export, import or permanently delete all One-Fill data from Import & export in the One-Fill settings page, clear history at any time, and turn history off entirely.
